One box. Everything goes in it.

How these get built

The same four rules, every time.

They're constraints, not slogans. Each one costs something, and each one is the reason a particular class of bug can't happen here.

01
It works offline. If an app needs a server to do its main job, it breaks in a parking garage. These don't — the thinking happens on the device.
02
No account. You shouldn't have to hand over an email address to print an envelope. Sign in only if you want your own calendar linked.
03
Your data stays yours. Nothing is uploaded to be read, scored or trained on. There is no analytics SDK collecting what you type.
04
It says so when it can't. An app that fails silently is worse than one that admits it. When something doesn't reach your calendar, you're told, in the words of the failure.
