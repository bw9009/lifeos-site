Common questions

  • Where did my thought go? Tap the filter chips (All, Reminders, Shopping…) above the list — everything you file lands in one of them. Notes and ideas live behind the notes icon up top.
  • Why didn't it hit my calendar? Calendar sync needs a connected Google account — the gear icon opens Connections. Only dated events sync; reminders ring on your phone instead.
  • A reminder didn't ring. Check that notifications are allowed for Life in Android settings, and that battery optimization isn't set to restrict it.
  • How do I change something I filed? Just type it: "move the dentist to 3pm", "cancel the vitamin reminder". Life edits the original.
