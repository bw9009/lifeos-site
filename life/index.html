iPhone

Join the iPhone public beta tester group

What TestFlight is, and why you need it. TestFlight is Apple's own app — published by Apple, free, on the App Store. It's the only way Apple allows anyone to install an app that hasn't finished its App Store release yet, so every iPhone beta on earth goes through it. Life is in that stage right now.

Apple reviews every build before it reaches you, the same review the App Store runs. Nothing is sideloaded, nothing bypasses Apple's protections, and you're not granting anything unusual. If you've ever beta-tested an iPhone app, this is the identical process.

Tap the button on your iPhone. If you don't have TestFlight, the App Store offers it first; then Life installs with one more tap. No account with us, no group to join, no invite code. Delete either one whenever you want.

The beta is capped at 1,000 testers — that's Apple's limit on a public link, and it's first come, first served. Once it fills, the link stops letting people in until spots open up. Claim yours now.

How it works

Fast, private, and it works on a bad signal.

Your thoughts stay on your phone. Sorting happens on-device — nothing is uploaded to be read, scored, or trained on.
