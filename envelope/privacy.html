Easy Print + Share Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

The short version: Easy Print + Share Studio collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no account. Everything you type or import stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no server that belongs to us. The app has no login because there is nothing to log in to.

What stays on your device

  • Your return address and layout. Saved locally so you don't retype it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • The recipient address. Held only while you're using the app to build that envelope.
  • Any postage image you import. Read from your gallery, used to build the PDF, and never copied anywhere else.

Photo access

The app asks for access to your photos only when you tap to import postage, and only reads the single image you pick. It does not scan your library, and it does not upload the image.

Printing

Printing is handled by Android's own printing system, which talks to your printer over your local network. The PDF is built on your phone and handed to Android directly. We never see it. If you print through a service that has its own cloud component (some manufacturer print apps do), that service's privacy policy applies to that step, not ours.

Children

Easy Print + Share Studio is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date above changes with it. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that would be stated here plainly and asked for in the app first.
