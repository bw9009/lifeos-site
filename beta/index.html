Questions people actually ask

Does it cost anything?

No. Testing is free, always, and nothing is ever charged to a tester.

What do you do with my data?

Nothing — there's nothing to do. Life has no accounts and no server of mine. What you type stays on your phone unless you connect your own Google Calendar or Tasks, and that goes to Google, not to me.

Will it break?

Sometimes. That's what beta means, and it's why the in-app "something off here" button exists — it writes the report for you, two taps to send. Those reports are the single most useful thing a tester can do.

Do I lose my stuff when the real version ships?

No. Your data lives on your device and carries over.

The Android link says the app isn't available.

Almost always one of two things. Either the join email went from a different address than the Google account on your phone — that's the common one — or Google just hasn't caught up yet. Check the account first, then give it a few minutes and reload.
