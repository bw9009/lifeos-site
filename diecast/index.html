How they're made

  • Designed in-house. Each scale is drawn from real tire sizes, not resized from a single model.
  • Printed in tough PETG, which holds up better than standard PLA and won't crack when a car rolls into it.
  • Measured before shipping. Every batch gets a caliper check against the listed dimensions.
  • Ships in one business day from Las Vegas, Nevada.
